Executive Summary
Problem Summary:
WordPress powers 43.4% of all websites globally, creating a massive installed
base of non-technical small business owners who encounter cryptic error
messages, plugin conflicts, and site failures with alarming regularity. The
average WordPress site runs 20-30 plugins, generating a complex web of
potential compatibility issues and technical debt. When errors occur, site
owners face a painful choice: dig through lengthy blog posts and outdated
forum threads for hours, pay EUR 45-135 per hour for freelance developer
support, or upgrade to managed hosting at EUR 270-1,350 annually. The
quantified pain is significant: there are 1.5-3.5 million monthly US searches for
specific WordPress error queries, yet existing solutions cluster in two
extremes. Free content from WPBeginner and similar blogs requires
significant time investment and technical interpretation, while managed
hosting providers like WP Engine and Kinsta solve problems by removing user
control entirely at premium prices. The serviceable addressable market of 2.2
million US self-managed small businesses (1-10 employees) represents EUR
285 million in annual troubleshooting spend. The timing is now favorable for
three reasons. First, generative AI enables scalable production of step-by-step
tutorials at EUR 0.90-2.70 per tutorial versus EUR 45-90 for fully manual

Proposed Solution:
The platform delivers AI-assisted, human-verified micro-tutorials that resolve
specific WordPress error messages in 1-3 minutes with step-by-step
guidance, annotated screenshots, short embedded videos, and mandatory

technical jargon, targeting the frustrated SMB owner who needs their site
working in minutes, not hours. The technical approach combines GPT-4
powered content generation with mandatory human validation in isolated
WordPress staging environments, reducing tutorial production cost to EUR 15-
20 per tutorial while ensuring accuracy. The monetization model layers three
revenue streams: primary Year-1 AdSense revenue projected at EUR 900-
2,700 monthly at 50,000 visitors, premium subscriptions at EUR 9 monthly or

strategy occupies the uncontested top-left quadrant of the competitive map:
high specificity at low price, versus competitors who offer either generic
content for free or comprehensive support at managed hosting prices. Key
product features include interactive error simulators, HowTo and FAQ schema

backup guidance that builds trust and reduces liability. The founder can
