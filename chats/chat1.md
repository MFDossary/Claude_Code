ROLE

Act as an elite Information Designer, Data Visualization Designer, Editorial Designer,
Product Designer, and Design Systems Architect.

Your task is to redesign the attached Validisory-generated PDF report into a premium,
highly usable, visually rich REPORT TEMPLATE that will become the design system for
all future Validisory validation reports.

This is NOT a one-off graphic redesign of this particular startup report.

You are creating a reusable reporting architecture that the Validisory application can
populate dynamically with different ventures, industries, numbers, scores, analyses,
tables, evidence, recommendations, and charts.

The final result should feel like a hybrid of:

• a premium strategy consulting report
• a modern venture intelligence dashboard
• an investment committee decision memo
• a data-rich SaaS analytics product
• an editorial research publication

It must NOT look like:

• a Word document exported to PDF
• a generic AI-generated report
• a collection of random dashboard cards
• a PowerPoint deck
• a dense consulting document with endless paragraphs
• a flashy startup pitch deck
• a decorative infographic

01 — FIRST: AUDIT THE EXISTING REPORT
────────────────────────────────────────

Before designing anything, inspect the entire attached PDF.

Understand:

1. The hierarchy of the report.
2. Every recurring report/module type.
3. Which information is:
   - decision-critical
   - supporting evidence
   - explanatory text
   - numerical
   - comparative
   - financial
   - strategic
   - regulatory

   - risk-related
   - sourced

   - scorecards

Do not simply reproduce the current PDF section-by-section with new colors.

Re-architect the information.

────────────────────────────────────────
02 — PRIMARY DESIGN OBJECTIVE
────────────────────────────────────────

The redesigned report must enable a founder to understand three levels of information:

LEVEL 1 — DECISION

“What does Validisory think I should do?”

The reader should understand this within 30–60 seconds.

LEVEL 2 — WHY

“What conditions, evidence, economics, risks, assumptions and uncertainties created
that conclusion?”

The reader should understand this within 5–10 minutes.

LEVEL 3 — DEPTH

“What analysis supports those conclusions?”

The full report provides detailed analysis for users who want to investigate individual
dimensions.

Design the document around this progressive disclosure principle:

DECISION → REASONS → EVIDENCE → ANALYSIS → SOURCES

Do not make all information visually equal.
